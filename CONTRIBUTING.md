# Contributing to Dopamine-OS

[Project home](README.md) · [Telegram @csakwild91](https://t.me/csakwild91)

This repository starts as a research proposal. Contributions that clarify, test, or refute the hypothesis are as welcome as contributions that advance it.

## Useful contributions now

- Scientific review with citations, clear objections, and a narrower falsifiable question.
- Pointers to existing datasets, including provenance, sensor metadata, and reuse permissions.
- Proposed data alignment, quality checks, and evaluation designs grounded in available data.
- Corrections to translations that preserve the founder's identity and the concept-stage status.
- Introductions to qualified scientific leads, laboratories, funding partners, and engineering collaborators.

Public review belongs in [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/rgamingbc/Dopamine-OS/issues). Private investment and resource enquiries belong on [Telegram](https://t.me/csakwild91). Share your expertise, what you could contribute, and the question or milestone you want to address.

## Evidence and rights

Clearly label established results, proposed hypotheses, and personal opinions. Include sources when making scientific claims. Do not present an AI-generated statement as experimental evidence.

Contribute only material you have the right to share. Do not upload sensitive personal information, private medical records, credentials, or unpublished data without permission. Third-party datasets require their own documented licenses and access conditions.

Original documentation contributions are intended for the repository's [CC BY 4.0 license](LICENSE); contributors retain authorship and receive attribution. Proposed software or datasets need an explicit licensing decision before inclusion.

Any experimental work must be led by qualified institutions under the required approvals. The initial repository does not provide or solicit instructions for self-experimentation, implantation, genetic manipulation, or unsupervised treatment.

Treat participants, patients, and researchers with respect. Challenge claims precisely and explain what evidence would change your conclusion.
